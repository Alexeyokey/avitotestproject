Quality decision
----------------
There is no clear evidence to replace the original document representation:
the effect is small, the exploratory interval spans losses and gains, and
compact trades103 newly found positive pairs for86 lost ones. A smaller
average pool does not by itself establish higher final ranking quality.
The original document format/current query remains the current production
reference. The V8 pipeline continues to use that original format.

The token-retention profile provided a plausible MECHANISM HYPOTHESIS for
compression, not an improvement guarantee. Actual candidate metrics did not
show a convincing direct replacement gain. These results also do not prove
that full descriptions or longer tokens are beneficial; length stayed128.

Newly found pairs suggest possible complementary information, but a protected
addition/ensemble would be a DIFFERENT hypothesis with its own capped allocation,
not the replacement measured here. No such extra test, second index, raw-score
feature update or reranker training was silently added to this experiment.

Scope and reproducibility
-------------------------
Candidate Recall is a pool upper bound; no compact-pool reranker Recall@50,
official hidden score or independent generalization improvement was measured.
All statistics belong to the sampled cold research catalog. The completed
Audit labels/report were never opened or used to choose the layout.
Only new configs/reports were written; frozen producer/evaluator/helper/dense
and token-profile source bytes were preserved.
